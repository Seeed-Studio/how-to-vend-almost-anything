Your lab makes it local.

A shared foundation gives each lab room to make the machine its own. The improvements become a better starting point for everyone.

  • Start with XIAO.

    A common board family gives makers an accessible starting point. Each lab confirms its own product availability.

  • Vend what fits your community.

    Choose components, learning kits, and things you make. Product dimensions and a compatible dispenser determine what the machine can carry.

  • Share what makes it better.

    Publish reusable machine designs, changes, source files, and lessons. Keep versions and compatibility clear so another lab can build on your work.
